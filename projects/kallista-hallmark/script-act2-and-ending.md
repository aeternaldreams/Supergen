# Kallista Vane holiday trailer: montage, cast roll and ending (draft 2)

Picks up right after the title cards "This winter... / ...love was in the last place she'd look."
The acoustic song bed carries straight through; dialogue sits on top of it.

## Working title options (undecided)
The title should be about Kallista's reservations, not only the meet-cute.
1. **Holiday Reservations**: her B&B booking and her guard against love. Tagline: "She made a reservation. She has several."
2. **Mistletoe & Misgivings**
3. **Love, Subsequently**: a nod to her song "Subsequent Consequence"
4. **The Worst That Could Happen**

Network: **DPC, the Deep Prompt Channel** (a play on Deep Prompt Cinema).

---

## MONTAGE: "Getting to know each other"
Rule: they keep running into each other at the same few places (café, B&B porch, town square).
Kallista stays in the same outfit plus a winter coat and scarf; Cal in flannel and a work jacket.

**M1: CAFÉ, MORNING (~5s)**
Both reach for the same cup at the pickup counter. The man-bun barista from the flash is in the background (callback).
BARISTA (off-screen): "Cal! ...And, uh, Callistuh!"
Their hands touch on the same cup. Kallista slowly turns it so she can read the name. It says "CAL."
CAL: "...Close enough?"

**M2: B&B FRONT PORCH, MORNING (~5s)**
Kallista opens the front door with her coffee. Cal is kneeling on the porch steps in a tool belt, replacing a board.
KALLISTA: "Are you following me?"
CAL: (holds up the hammer) "I was hired... in August."

**M3: JOB SITE, DAY (~4s)**
Dusty eats a sandwich on a sawhorse while Cal measures a board.
DUSTY: "Dark dream-pop. That's just pop... that didn't get enough sleep."
CAL: (not looking up) "You looked her up."
DUSTY: "I've listened to it nine times."

**M4: B&B WINDOW, DAY (~3s)**
Becca and Milly at the window, watching Cal on the porch. Becca clutches the curtain, swooning.
MILLY: (flat, doesn't blink) "I give it... ninety minutes."
(IYKYK: the runtime of every one of these movies.)

**M5: TOWN SQUARE HOLIDAY FAIR, DUSK (~6s), the emotional turn**
Snow falls. Kallista plays a few notes on an old, out-of-tune upright piano left out for the fair. Cal walks up behind her.
CAL: "I could fix that."
KALLISTA: (keeps playing, softly) "Some things are supposed to sound... a little broken."
Cal looks at her and doesn't have a joke this time.
The music swells here.

**M6: SIDEWALK OUTSIDE THE LAUNDROMAT, NIGHT (~5s)**
Kallista slips on ice (callback to the rink); Cal catches her by the arms. They hold the moment.
KALLISTA: "I don't do... falling."
CAL: (still holding her) "What's the worst that could happen?"
KALLISTA: (deadpan, completely sincere) "Uh... you could die?"
Cal blinks, stunned. A beat. He doesn't let go.
CAL: "...Okay. But besides that?"
She laughs for real for the first time.

## Supergen clip plan (max 4 spoken lines per clip, about 2.5 words/sec)
- **Clip A (15s):** M1 + M2 (4 lines). Café and porch are different setups, so hard cut at ~6s.
- **Clip B (10s):** M3 + M4 (4 lines). The Dusty and Milly lines are short and fit.
- **Clip C (10s):** M5 (2 lines). Slow, emotional, Golden Hour seed.
- **Clip D (10–15s):** M6 (4 lines, the max). End on the silent laugh (non-verbal last shot, per the speech-queue rule).
- **Cast roll:** 5-second single-image pickups, one per character. Dusty's is the only one with a spoken line.
- **Stinger:** 5-second pickup. Bossa nova and "WOO!" added in the edit, not generated.
- Title and name cards: from Claude, overlaid in the edit.

## Prompting note: no static effects
Keep "static", "crackle" and "spark" out of every Supergen prompt. Models tend to overdo them (visible lightning, glowing sparks) and it looks corny. The bra on Cal's back is explained by the dialogue alone. If a small crackle sound is wanted, add it quietly in the edit.
