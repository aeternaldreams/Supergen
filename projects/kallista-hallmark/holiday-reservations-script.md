# HOLIDAY RESERVATIONS: full trailer script (draft 4)

A DPC (Deep Prompt Channel) original. No narrator: the story is told through dialogue, Claude title cards and a soft acoustic song bed.

The structure is from the Grokbot handoff:
1. **Part 1, Cold open (unchanged from the Grokbot handoff).** The three friends on the B&B couch, Becca's guesses with the white-flash fake meet-cutes, then the real laundromat meet-cute.
2. **Part 2, Montage (new).**
3. **Part 3, Ensemble cast roll (from the handoff), title, end card and stinger.**

The only change to Part 1: the "static crackles" directions are removed (see the prompting note at the end).

# PART 1: COLD OPEN (Grokbot handoff, unchanged)

**COLD OPEN: B&B SITTING ROOM, NIGHT**
A fire crackles. Kallista is curled up on the couch with a glass of red, Becca has the whole bottle, and Milly sits perfectly upright in an armchair with an untouched glass.

KALLISTA: So... something kind of weird happened today.
BECCA: (gasps, sets the bottle down) No. Don't tell me. You met someone.
KALLISTA: I didn't say—
BECCA: The ice rink. Hockey player. Dimples. Probably named Chase.

**WHITE FLASH.** An outdoor town rink with twinkle lights. Kallista sits on the bench lacing up her skates. A cocky hockey player races toward her and does a big hockey stop that blasts a wall of ice shavings into her face. Her arms fly up in a "what the hell?!" flinch. He grins.
CHASE: Hey...
**WHITE FLASH.**

Back on the couch, Kallista gives Becca a slow, dead-eyed head shake.

BECCA: The coffee shop! He spelled your name wrong on purpose so you'd have to talk to him.

**WHITE FLASH.** A cozy café. A barista with a man bun turns the cup around. It says "CALLISTUH" with a heart. Kallista stares at it. **WHITE FLASH.**

KALLISTA: (sips wine) Nobody does that.
BECCA: Ooh, the music shop! Some broody musician.

**WHITE FLASH.** A music store. A Yamaha PSR-SX920 arranger keyboard sits on a stand. Kallista stands at one end, studying the panel, her finger hovering over a key. Adrian Vale glides in from the far end and sets his elbow down on the panel to rest his chin on his hand. His elbow presses the style start; a quiet count-in tick... tick... starts underneath.
ADRIAN: (British, low and velvety) You play? (tick... tick...) Perhaps we could make something... beautifully dark—
BOOM. A full cheesy bossa nova beat blasts in and cuts off "together." Adrian jolts upright and panics, his eyes darting all over the panel as he jabs buttons, twists knobs and slides faders. Every touch makes it worse: the tempo speeds up, a horn section joins in and a "WOO!" fires. Kallista just watches. **WHITE FLASH.**

KALLISTA: (deadpan, sips wine) He was perfect... until the bossa nova.
MILLY: (flat, not blinking) Statistically, most people meet at work.
Both of them turn and stare at Milly. A long beat.
BECCA: (back to Kallista) Then where?!
KALLISTA: (sinks into the couch) ...The laundromat.
BECCA: That's adorable!
KALLISTA: My bra was stuck to his back.

**THE REAL MEET-CUTE: SMALL-TOWN LAUNDROMAT, DAY**
Kallista, in the same outfit as the flashes, pulls warm laundry out of a dryer. Cal folds his towels nearby and turns around. One of her lacy black bras is clinging to the back of his flannel.

KALLISTA: Um... excuse me. You have something.
CAL: (patting his shoulders, turning in circles like a dog chasing its tail) Where? Is it a bug?
KALLISTA: It's... mine.
He finally reaches back, peels it off, and holds it up between them. They both freeze.
CAL: (trying very hard to be a gentleman) I'd like to say this has never happened to me before.
KALLISTA: (snatching it back) Has it?
CAL: Not with one this nice.
She almost smiles, catches herself, and leaves. Cal watches her go and folds the same towel three times.

**TITLE CARD:** This winter...
**TITLE CARD:** ...love was in the last place she'd look.

# CHARACTER AND WARDROBE NOTES

## Kallista in this story
Lines marked (new) are written for this trailer; they are not existing Kallista quotes.
- **Wants:** a quiet getaway with her friends, with nothing to lose.
- **What stops her:** she's convinced warmth doesn't last ("Uh... you could die?").
- **The contradiction:** she keeps going back to the café at the exact same time.
- **Last open question:** the half-open door. She doesn't close it and she doesn't walk through it yet. (This borrows the half-open door from Inviolate Fixation, softened to fit Hallmark.)
- **Performance:** restraint. Delayed glances, almost-smiles she catches herself on, short direct lines.
- **Song easter eggs:** Dusty mangles "Subsequent Consequence"; Becca uses "Don't Follow" against her; the last card nods to "The Thaw Inside the Bone."

## Wardrobe
- **Outfit A (cold open flashes + real laundromat, locked):** black ribbed mock-neck sweater (identity shot 1). Same outfit in every flash, because she's the one telling the story; only the guy changes.
- **Couch scenes (cold open + M4):** cozy evening version, e.g. an oversized charcoal knit, so the couch reads as "now" and the flashes read as "that day".
- **Montage (different days):** rotate within her palette (black, charcoal, grey) so the days read as days: the charcoal turtleneck (identity shot 2), a dark cardigan, Outfit A again. Outdoors: the same charcoal wool coat and dark grey scarf throughout. One coat across a winter trip is normal and helps continuity.
- Hair is always the same: platinum, straight, worn down, just past the shoulders.
- No slip dresses (those belong to her sultrier music videos).

# PART 2: MONTAGE (new)

## "Getting to know each other"
Rule: they keep running into each other at the same few places (café, B&B porch, town square).
Cal wears flannel and a work jacket.

**M1: CAFÉ, MORNING (~5s)**
Both reach for the same cup at the pickup counter. The man-bun barista from the flash is in the background (callback).
BARISTA (off-screen): "Cal! ...And, uh, Callistuh!"
Their hands touch on the same cup. Kallista slowly turns it so she can read the name. It says "CAL."
CAL: "...Close enough?"

**M2: B&B FRONT PORCH, MORNING (~5s)**
Kallista opens the front door with her coffee. Cal is kneeling on the porch steps in a tool belt, replacing a board.
KALLISTA: "Are you following me?"
CAL: (holds up the hammer) "I was hired... in August."

**M3: JOB SITE, DAY (~8s)**
Dusty eats a sandwich on a sawhorse while Cal measures a board.
DUSTY: "Dark dream-pop. That's just pop... that didn't get enough sleep."
CAL: (not looking up) "You looked her up."
DUSTY: "'Subsequential Consequences.' Nine times. Total banger."
CAL: "...That's not what it's called."

**M4: B&B SITTING ROOM, DAY (~6s)** (new: shows her choosing)
Kallista comes in from the cold holding a café cup. Becca is waiting on the couch with her arms crossed. Milly sits upright in the armchair.
BECCA: "Third day. Same café. Same time."
KALLISTA: (sets the cup down, avoids eye contact) "...The scones are good."
BECCA: "You wrote a whole song called 'Don't Follow.'"
MILLY: (flat, doesn't blink) "I give it... ninety minutes."
(IYKYK: the runtime of every one of these movies.)

**M5: TOWN SQUARE HOLIDAY FAIR, DUSK (~6s), the emotional turn**
Snow falls. Kallista plays a few notes on an old, out-of-tune upright piano left out for the fair. Cal walks up behind her.
CAL: "I could fix that."
KALLISTA: (keeps playing, softly) "Some things are supposed to sound... a little broken."
Cal sits down next to her on the bench and doesn't make a joke this time. She lets him stay.
The music swells here.

**M6: SIDEWALK OUTSIDE THE LAUNDROMAT, NIGHT (~5s)**
Kallista slips on ice (callback to the rink); Cal catches her by the arms. They hold the moment.
KALLISTA: "I don't do... falling."
CAL: (still holding her) "What's the worst that could happen?"
KALLISTA: (deadpan, completely sincere) "Uh... you could die?"
Cal blinks, stunned. A beat. He doesn't let go.
CAL: "...Okay. But besides that?"
She laughs for real for the first time.

**M7: B&B FRONT DOOR, NIGHT (~4s), last story image, no dialogue**
Cal walks her back to the porch. She says goodnight, goes inside and starts to close the door... then stops with it half open. Through the gap she watches him walk down the snowy path. He looks back. Almost-smile.

**TITLE CARD:** She came for a quiet getaway...
**TITLE CARD:** ...not a thaw.

ADRIAN: (quietly, to himself) "...Beautifully dark."

## Supergen clip plan (max 4 spoken lines per clip, about 2.5 words/sec)
- **Clip A (15s):** M1 + M2 (4 lines). Café and porch are different setups, so hard cut at ~6s.
- **Clip B (10s):** M3 (4 lines), job site.
- **Clip B2 (10s):** M4 (4 lines), sitting room.
- **Clip C (10s):** M5 (2 lines). Slow, emotional, Golden Hour seed.
- **Clip D (10–15s):** M6 (4 lines, the max). End on the silent laugh (non-verbal last shot, per the speech-queue rule).
- **Clip E (5s):** M7, silent, one image.
- **Cast roll:** 5-second single-image pickups, one per character. Dusty's is the only one with a spoken line.
- **Stinger:** 5-second pickup. Bossa nova and "WOO!" added in the edit, not generated.
- Title and name cards: from Claude, overlaid in the edit.

## Prompting note: no static effects
Keep "static", static "crackle" and "spark" out of every Supergen prompt (the fireplace crackle in the cold open is fine). Models tend to overdo them (visible lightning, glowing sparks) and it looks corny. The bra on Cal's back is explained by the dialogue alone. If a small crackle sound is wanted, add it quietly in the edit.
